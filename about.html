Our purpose

What we’re here to do.

We use community radio to promote the entire Jaman area, share local knowledge and bring people together to address the issues that affect them.

01 / FARMING

Support farmers

Give cashew growers and other farmers airtime to share practical advice, raise concerns and find out about available support.

02 / CULTURE

Promote local culture

Share Jaman’s traditions, local languages, music, stories and cultural events on air.

03 / COMMUNITY

Address social issues

Bring residents together to discuss local problems and practical ways to respond.

04 / EDUCATION

Help students

Share learning opportunities, useful announcements and information for students and families.

05 / SUPPORT

Reach people in need

Give people facing hardship a way to raise concerns and share information about help in the community.

06 / ENTERPRISE

Promote local businesses

Help local entrepreneurs introduce their products and services to listeners.
